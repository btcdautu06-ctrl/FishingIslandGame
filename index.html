🌴 Angler's Island Field Guide

🏝️ Archipelago Exploration & 240+ Trees

Walk across the islands using W, A, S, D and sprint with Shift. Drag the mouse to look around in full 3D!

The world spans 4 Archipelago Islands (Main Haven, Tropical Coral Atoll, Volcanic Pine Crags, and Titan Leviathan Atoll) connected by arched wooden suspension bridges. Over 240 trees sway in the coastal wind! Press E near any tree to shake it for bait, coconuts, apples, and gold coins!

☠️ Deadly Water Rule

The open oceans and lakes are strictly lethal—touching water means instant death! Stay safely on land, docks, or on the elevated wooden suspension bridges. Walking off a bridge or stepping into the ocean will drown you immediately!

⚓ Bait Market & Island Deeds

Visit Captain Barnaby's Shop (E) to buy bait in bulk (x1, x5, or x20 with bulk discounts) or purchase Sea Charters / Deeds to new islands! Once unlocked, charter Barnaby's Ferry for instant travel, or walk across the grand suspension bridges!

🎣 How to Catch Colossal Titans

  1. Walk to the edge of the water (ocean shores, deep ocean pier, hot spring, or pond).
  2. Hold Space or click & hold CAST ROD to charge distance, then release!
  3. Watch the water! Big fish cast huge underwater shadows. When an exclamation "❗ BITE!" appears, press Space to hook!
  4. Reel Minigame: Hold Space or Left Mouse Button to keep the green bar over the fish. Watch line tension! Colossal Titans like the Megalodon Rex, Kraken, and Great White Shark fight fiercely!

☀️ Time of Day & Habitats

Rest at the campfire or click the Time pill on your HUD to switch between Dawn, Day, Sunset, and Night. Apex beasts and bioluminescent legends emerge under starry skies!
